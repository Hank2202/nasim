The Summit of Realms

A real-time, offline-first geopolitical strategy simulation. Six sovereign nations convene at the Neutral Spire to navigate resource scarcity, conduct bilateral trade, author statutory motions, and fulfill classified domestic directives over three operational turns.

The Neutral Spire & The Great Convergence

Three centuries after the cataclysmic tectonic upheaval known as the Great Convergence, continental plates fused inward, elevating the obsidian cliffs of the Neutral Spire. Around this central sanctum lie six distinct sovereign nations with fundamentally asymmetrical endowments—ranging from fertile agrarian lowlands and volcanic smelters to maritime trade straits and abyssal mining chasms.

With harvest shortfalls looming, the six sovereign delegations have convened at the Spire of Accords for a decisive three-turn diplomatic summit. Every nation must navigate a demanding Triple Mandate:

I. Domestic Survival

Prevent famine at all costs. Populations consume 20 Alchemical Grain every turn (15 if Technological Efficiency is unlocked). Silos reaching 0 trigger severe civil starvation riots (-25 Vitality, -20 Stability).

II. Continental Governance

Leverage Diplomatic Standing to author and pass statutory resolutions—including humanitarian grain transfers, wealth surtaxes, and border quarantines—to shape continental order.

III. Classified Directives

Each sovereign nation harbors a secret strategic objective accessible only to their delegation terminal. Achieving this classified mandate before Turn 3 concludes secures sovereign victory.

Simulation Chronology (3 Turns)

The simulation runs through 3 operational turns. Each turn combines freeform bilateral negotiations and formal parliamentary plenary sessions:

Phase 1: Working & Bilateral Trade Session (5 Minutes) ACTIVE ROOM FLOOR

Delegates move freely through the room conducting face-to-face negotiations. Via smartphone terminals, delegations execute atomic P2P trades, trigger internal domestic policies (relief, forges, galas), read historical dossiers for quiz rewards, and author statutory motions for the Plenary docket.

Phase 2: Plenary Floor & Flash Assembly (3–5 Minutes) PARLIAMENTARY ROLL-CALL

Delegations return to their seats. Statutory motions that secured a Second Endorsement are called to the floor. Nations cast weighted votes (1, 2, or 3 votes). If affirmative votes exceed negative votes, the statute is enacted immediately across all nations.

Phase 3: Turn Processing & Socioeconomic Settlement ENGINE EXECUTION

The engine deducts grain consumption (-20 or -15), executes starvation penalties if silos empty, evaluates the Complacency Paradox, awards the Plutocratic bonus vote, recalculates voting weights, and publishes continental news dispatches.

Turn 3 Finale: Summit Concluded & Debriefing STRATEGIC REVELATION

At the conclusion of Turn 3, the facilitator triggers simulation conclusion. Secret directives are unveiled, continental awards (Hegemon, Plutocrat, Shield) are tallied, and final rankings are broadcasted on the auditorium projector.

Socioeconomic Algorithms & Formulas

The simulation engine executes transparent, deterministic socioeconomic equations at each turn settlement:

Dynamic Voting Weights
Standing ≥ 80 → 3 Votes
Standing 45–79 → 2 Votes
Standing < 45 → 1 Vote
+1 Bonus Vote for Continental Plutocrat

Derived directly from national diplomatic standing. High standing confers assembly veto power and multilateral influence.

Baseline Consumption & Famine
Turn Consumption = -20 Grain (Standard)
With Tech Efficiency = -15 Grain (-25% cut)
If Grain ≤ 0: -25 Vitality, -20 Stability

Civil populations consume grain every turn. Running out of grain triggers immediate domestic starvation riots.

Technological Efficiency
Threshold: Aether Cores ≥ 75
Effect: Permanent -25% Grain Consumption
Status: Broadcasted to Continental News Wire

Industrializing with 75+ active aether matrices unlocks automation that permanently cuts food consumption from 20 to 15.

Plutocratic Drift
Condition: Highest Tangible Wealth (Grain+Cores+Silk)
Reward: +1 Assembly Vote
Penalty: -5 Civil Stability per Turn

The single wealthiest nation gains an extra vote from sheer financial clout, but domestic inequality erodes stability.

The Complacency Paradox
Condition: Vitality ≥ 85 AND Stability ≥ 85
Effect: -10% Domestic Output Slump
Remedy: Expending Echo Silk (Hosting Grand Galas)

When populations grow overly comfortable, productivity slumps unless luxury silk is expended to maintain social energy.

Qualitative Fog of War Tiers
≥ 60: Abundant / Surplus / Lavish
25–59: Sufficient / Operational / Modest
< 25: Depleted / Scarce / Negligible

Projector and foreign registries show qualitative tiers rather than exact values to preserve negotiation tension and bluffing.

Internal Domestic Decrees

During Working Sessions, delegations can enact internal state decrees on their mobile cockpit to convert surplus resources into national health, security, or military strength:

Emergency Grain Rationing (Relief)
Releases stored alchemical grain from national silos to domestic breadlines.
-15 Grain → +15 Public Vitality
Stoke Arcane Forges (Fabrication)
Mobilizes industrial blast furnaces using grain fuel and silk insulation.
-10 Grain, -10 Silk → +15 Aether Cores
Host Grand Cultural Gala (Diplomacy)
Hosts lavish banquets with gossamer silk to elevate international prestige and social calm.
-15 Silk → +10 Standing, +10 Stability
Mobilize Civil Guard (Security)
Powers regional security wardens with aether batteries to suppress domestic riots.
-10 Cores → +15 Civil Stability
Muster Sovereign Regiments (Military)
Levies knightly battalions and fortifies territorial garrisons.
-10 Grain, -10 Cores → +15 Military Power
Statutory Motions & Two-Sponsor Mandate

Any nation can author a statutory declaration, but to prevent filibustering, a motion only qualifies for a plenary vote once a different sovereign nation seconds it:

HUMANITARIAN GRAIN TRANSFER

Emergency Grain Redistribution

Mandates wealthy grain silos contribute emergency food to starving provinces.

Realms with >50 Grain lose -10 Grain; realms with <25 Grain gain +15 Grain.
PLUTOCRATIC RESTRAINTS

Continental Wealth Surtax & Embargo

Imposes commercial trade sanctions on runaway oligarchic dominance.

Wealthiest realm suffers -15 Civil Stability as trade routes are choked.
DIPLOMATIC ELEVATION

Sovereign Relief Mission

Multilateral relief initiative organized by the sponsoring delegation.

Sponsoring realm gains +10 Diplomatic Standing for international leadership.
BORDER QUARANTINE

Frontier Defense & Quarantine Cordon

Militarizes frontier checkpoints to insulate against refugee surges or crisis.

Targeted realm borders quarantined (blocks foreign trade) and gains +10 Stability.
Knowledge Dossiers & Trivia Quizzes

Delegations can access 8 historical dossiers in the Lore & Intel mobile tab. Each contains a high-stakes quiz:

  • Correct Answer: Awards an instant bounty of +15 Resources (Grain, Cores, or Silk) directly to national reserves.
  • Incorrect Answer: Incurs a penalty of -5 Diplomatic Standing and permanently locks the dossier.
