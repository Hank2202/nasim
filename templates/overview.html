1. The Neutral Spire & The Great Convergence

HISTORICAL SETTING

Three centuries after the cataclysmic tectonic upheaval known as the Great Convergence, the continental plates fused inward, creating the obsidian plateaus of the Neutral Spire. Around this central sanctum lie six disparate sovereign nations, each forged by contrasting environments—from volcanic smelters and misty sea straits to fungal lowlands and sun-baked marble plateaus.

Now, a continental harvest deficit and escalating technological disparity threaten total destabilization. The six delegations have convened at the Spire of Accords for a mandatory Three-Turn Summit. Every sovereign delegation faces a demanding Triple Mandate:

🌾 I. Preserve Domestic Survival

Prevent catastrophic famine. Populations steadily consume 20 Alchemical Grain every turn. Allowing food silos to drop to zero unleashes severe humanitarian collapse (-25 Vitality, -20 Stability).

🏛️ II. Shape Continental Statutes

Leverage Diplomatic Standing to author and vote on statutory resolutions—such as emergency grain aid, plutocratic wealth sanctions, or border cordons—that redistribute wealth and impose international order.

🔒 III. Fulfill Classified Directives

Each realm harbors a secret, high-priority national objective known only to their delegation. Fulfilling your classified directive by the conclusion of Turn 3 secures historical triumph for your people.

2. Simulation Flow & Turn Structure

CHRONOLOGY

The simulation operates across 3 consecutive Turns. Each Turn follows a structured cadence combining high-energy physical negotiation and formal parliamentary plenary debate:

PHASE 1
Working & Bilateral Trading Session (5:00 Minutes) ACTIVE FLOOR

Participants freely roam the room conducting face-to-face bilateral diplomacy. Using their mobile consoles, delegates initiate atomic P2P trades, trigger internal domestic policies (relief, forges, galas), read historical dossiers for quiz rewards, and draft statutory motions for the Plenary docket.

PHASE 2
Plenary Floor Debate & Flash Assembly (3:00–5:00 Minutes) PARLIAMENTARY

All nations return to their delegation seats. Resolutions that secured the mandatory Second Sovereign Endorsement are called to the floor. Delegates cast weighted votes (1, 2, or 3 votes) in real-time. If YES > NO, the resolution immediately takes statutory effect.

PHASE 3
Turn Resolution & Socioeconomic Processing AUTOMATED

The engine automatically deducts grain consumption (-20 or -15), triggers famine penalties if silos are empty, evaluates the Complacency Paradox, awards the Plutocratic bonus vote, calculates dynamic voting weights, and broadcasts news alerts across all screens.

TURN 3
Summit Concluded: Revelation & Debrief FINALE

At the close of Turn 3, the facilitator concludes the simulation. Secret agendas are publicly unveiled, final continental rankings are displayed on the auditorium projector, and successful nations celebrate their strategic triumphs.

4. Economic Engine & Mathematical Formulas

RULES CHEAT SHEET

The simulation is governed by strict, transparent socioeconomic algorithms executed at the close of every operational turn:

🗳️ Dynamic Voting Weights
Standing ≥ 80 → 3 Votes
Standing 45–79 → 2 Votes
Standing < 45 → 1 Vote
+1 Bonus Vote for Continental Plutocrat

Your influence in the Plenary Assembly is directly tied to your sovereign moral standing and wealth supremacy.

🌾 Baseline Consumption & Famine
Turn Consumption = -20 Grain (Standard)
With Tech Efficiency = -15 Grain (-25% cut)
If Grain ≤ 0: -25 Vitality, -20 Stability

Populations demand feeding every turn. Running out of grain triggers immediate domestic starvation riots.

⚡ Technological Efficiency
Condition: Aether Cores ≥ 75
Effect: Permanent -25% Grain Consumption
Status: Broadcasted to Continental Wire

Industrializing with 75+ active energy matrices enables alchemical automation that permanently reduces food needs.

💰 Plutocratic Drift & Inequality
Condition: Highest Total Tangible Wealth
Tangible Wealth = (Grain + Cores + Silk)
Reward: +1 Assembly Vote
Penalty: -5 Civil Stability per Turn

Runaway wealth buys political leverage in the Spire, but breeds bitter domestic labor discontent at home.

🛋️ The Complacency Paradox
Condition: Vitality ≥ 85 AND Stability ≥ 85
Effect: -10% Output Slump each turn
Cure: Expending luxury Echo Silk (Grand Galas)

When populations grow too peaceful and comfortable, civic drive stagnates unless cultural stimulus is injected.

🌫️ Qualitative Fog of War Tiers
≥ 60: Abundant / Surplus / Lavish
25–59: Sufficient / Operational / Modest
< 25: Depleted / Scarce / Negligible

The auditorium projector and foreign dossiers display qualitative tiers rather than exact numbers to preserve tension and bluffing.

6. Parliamentary Law & Statutory Resolutions

PLENARY DOCKET

Any sovereign nation can sponsor a statutory declaration, but to prevent filibustering and chaotic docket clutter, the Spire enforces the strict Two-Country Endorsement Rule:

The Second Sponsor Mandate:

A motion remains a private draft until a different sovereign nation inspects it in their Declarations tab and clicks "Second Motion". Once seconded, the motion immediately advances to the Plenary Floor docket for a continental vote!

HUMANITARIAN GRAIN TRANSFER

Emergency Grain Redistribution

Mandates wealthy silos contribute food to avert continental famine.

Realms with >50 Grain lose -10 Grain; realms with <25 Grain receive +15 Grain.
PLUTOCRATIC RESTRAINTS

Continental Wealth Surtax & Embargo

Imposes coordinated commercial sanctions on runaway oligarchs.

The single wealthiest realm suffers -15 Civil Stability as trade routes are choked.
DIPLOMATIC ELEVATION

Sovereign Relief Mission

Multilateral humanitarian initiative spearheaded by the sponsoring delegation.

Sponsoring realm gains +10 Diplomatic Standing for leadership in the Spire.
BORDER QUARANTINE

Frontier Defense & Quarantine Cordon

Fortifies borders to insulate against refugee surges, famine spillover, or contagion.

Targeted realm borders quarantined (blocks foreign trade) and gains +10 Stability.

7. Knowledge Bounties & Geographic Hazards

INTEL & CATASTROPHES

📚 High-Stakes Lore Quizzes

In the mobile Lore & Intel tab, delegations can study 8 historical dossiers detailing Spire geography, faction science, and cultural history. Each dossier features an interactive high-stakes quiz:

  • Correct Answer: Awards an instant bounty of +15 Resources (Grain, Cores, or Silk) directly to national reserves.
  • Incorrect Answer: Suffers a diplomatic embarrassment penalty of -5 Diplomatic Standing and locks the dossier permanently.

🌋 Terrain-Linked Catastrophes

Disasters are not random—they strike according to terrain and geographic affinities:

  • Tsubduction Earthquakes: Strike volcanic peaks and abyssal chasms (Val-Khor, Umbra, Aethelgard).
  • Oceanic Tsunamis: Flood coastal archipelagos and lowlands (Aquila, Sylvan).
  • Agricultural Blights & Wildfires: Scorch verdant lowlands and solar terraces (Sylvan, Solaris).
  • Aether Reactor Breaches: Rupture energy synthesizers in high-core nations.
