5 · Random events

Events strike at random moments of each working session. Choosing a scenario fills in its suggested values; change them freely.

Each small item hits one nation (good or bad news) and moves each resource it touches by a random amount between 0 and the largest change. Large disasters (earthquakes, dragon raids, plagues…, usually −10 to −25) strike one nation and are meant for the Armageddon scenario; at 0 you can still trigger them by hand from the command deck.
