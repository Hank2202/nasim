War

Nations can attack each other from their phones. The result depends mostly on the armies, then on how fed, stable and supplied each side is, on the ground and the weather, and a little on luck.

Scenario numbers

Choosing a scenario fills these in; change them freely to make it gentler or harsher. Events strike at random moments of each working session.

Each small item hits one nation (good or bad news) and moves each resource it touches by a random amount between 0 and the largest change. Large disasters (earthquakes, dragon raids, plagues…, usually −10 to −25) strike one nation and are meant for the Armageddon scenario; at 0 you can still trigger them by hand from the command deck.
